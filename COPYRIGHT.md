# Copyright and Provenance

## Copyright Status

HRONOAZA Studio is currently maintained as a private development project.

No public license, assignment, waiver, or other general grant of copyright permissions is made by this repository unless such a grant is stated explicitly in a separate committed legal document.

Unless otherwise documented for a specific file or component, all applicable rights are reserved by their respective copyright holder(s).

## Ownership

The existence of this repository, a Git commit, account ownership, authorship metadata, or contribution history does not by itself resolve every legal question of copyright ownership.

Where ownership matters, it should be supported by the relevant evidence, which may include:

- original source materials;
- dated working files;
- commit history;
- signed statements or agreements;
- contribution records;
- assignment or employment agreements;
- release and publication records;
- independently verifiable timestamps or hashes.

No contributor, tool, service, or repository host should be assumed to have transferred ownership rights unless that transfer is documented explicitly.

## Provenance

The project should preserve enough provenance to identify, where practical:

- where a component originated;
- who introduced it into the current repository;
- the exact commit in which it entered the baseline;
- whether it was modified during migration or cleanup;
- any third-party source or license that applies;
- the evidence used to support authorship or ownership claims.

Historical source material may be preserved separately from the current baseline.

Importing historical material into this repository does not erase its earlier provenance, and cleaning or renaming a component does not create a new ownership claim by itself.

## Third-Party Material

Third-party code, media, documentation, fonts, data, models, dependencies, or other protected material must not be represented as original HRONOAZA Studio material.

When third-party material is introduced, its origin and applicable license or permission should be documented before distribution.

Where appropriate, third-party notices should be maintained in a dedicated file such as `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Contributions

A contribution to this repository does not automatically imply transfer of copyright ownership.

Any required contributor licensing, copyright assignment, employment ownership, or other rights arrangement should be documented separately and explicitly.

Until a contribution policy is adopted, contributions should be accepted only when their provenance and permission to use are sufficiently clear for the intended project use.

The absence of a committed public license means that no general permission is granted here to copy, modify, distribute, sublicense, or commercially exploit HRONOAZA Studio beyond rights that may apply independently under law or under the terms of the hosting platform.

Any future licensing decision should be made only after the relevant source, dependencies, provenance, ownership, and third-party obligations have been reviewed.

## Evidence Principle

Project claims concerning authorship, ownership, originality, licensing, security, or provenance should be tied to specific evidence and scope.

Where possible, important evidence should be anchored to immutable or independently verifiable identifiers such as commit SHAs, signed tags, release records, checksums, or trusted timestamps.
